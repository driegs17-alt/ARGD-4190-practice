Stuff They Don’t
Want You to Know

Brand identity and episode cover system for a podcast on conspiracies and unexplained history.

Role
Identity design
Context
University of Georgia
Tools
Photoshop, InDesign
Year
2026

The brief

Design a logotype and show cover for a podcast about what is missing from the official record, then extend it into a system. The goal was to avoid the genre’s usual grain, glitch and alarm red, and look credible instead.

From first draft to final cover

The core idea came first: a redaction bar in place of the word “Want.” The first draft used heavy capitals and bright red, which felt like a tabloid. The final cover keeps the composition and switches to an editorial serif and a calmer palette.

One template for every episode

Each episode cover uses the same frame. The story category sets the background color, and one keyword is placed in the redaction bar, so a new cover takes minutes rather than a new illustration.

In use

The final presentation shows the identity across the show cover, a listening app and a watch.

Concept work for a university design course. Not affiliated with the show or its publisher.
