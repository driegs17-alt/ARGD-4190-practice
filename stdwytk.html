Challenge

Conspiracy media all looks the same: grain, glitch, tinfoil and blood-red type. The show needed to look credible enough to trust, strange enough to click, and still read as a 60 px square in a podcast feed.

Insight

The show is about what’s been left out of the record. A redaction bar is the most recognizable sign of hidden information, and it’s already a strong graphic shape. It needed no illustration to explain it.

Solution

An identity that redacts itself. “Want” is blacked out on the show cover, brackets frame every file, and a new episode cover needs only a template, a title, and one decision: which word gets the bar.

Process

From one cover to a system.

01

The first cover

I started with type alone: heavy caps, a red bracket frame, and a black bar where “Want” should be. The idea landed right away, since the bar reads as censorship before you read a word.

The tone was the problem. Heavy caps and pure red felt like a tabloid headline, not an investigation.
