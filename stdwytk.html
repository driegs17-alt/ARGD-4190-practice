Brand Identity · 2026

Stuff They Don’t
Want You to Know

Role
Identity design, art direction
Context
Concept rebrand, University of Georgia
Deliverables
Show cover, episode cover system, guidelines
Tools
Photoshop, InDesign

A podcast about what is missing from the official record. The identity is built on the redaction bar and applies it to the show’s own name.

The goal was to move away from the genre’s usual cues, such as grain, glitch effects and alarm red, toward something credible and documentary that still reads clearly as a small thumbnail.

Process

The first cover established the idea: a bracket frame with a black bar in place of “Want.” Its heavy capitals and saturated red read as tabloid, so the refined version keeps the composition and moves to an editorial serif and a quieter palette.

I also explored illustrated episode covers built from redaction bars. They were strong individually, but a new illustration every week is not sustainable for a weekly show, so the final system uses a template instead.

Episode System

Every episode uses the same frame. The story category sets the background color, and one keyword in the title is placed in the redaction bar.

  • #0F1714
  • #1E3A2F
  • #C9CFC6
  • #E8603A
  • #ECE6D8

Application

The system on a presentation display, alongside the app and watch interfaces.

Concept work for a university design course. Not affiliated with the show or its publisher.
